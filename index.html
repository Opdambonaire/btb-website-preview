    Court rules

    Use the facility for free when you follow these rules. Please point them out to each other when needed.

    • Beach tennis only.
    • Keep the beach clean.
    • Turn off the lights after playing.
    • Make sure everyone can play: rotate after each game.
    • Take good care of our nets, lines and lights.
    • Playing and entering the courts is at your own risk.
    • Kindly point these rules out to each other.
    • Have fun!

      How it works

      Points come from ranking tournaments and from the league.

      All participants in ranking tournaments are placed on the BTB ranking list automatically. League results also count towards the Bonaire Ranking System; the points per round are set out in the league regulations.

      The national doubles ranking also decides who represents Bonaire at the Pan American Championships 2026.

      How the league, the tournaments and the PanAm selection work.

      LeagueTournamentsPanAm 2026

      League regulations 2026

      Questions about the league: league@beachtennisbonaire.com

      General information

      Participation in the league costs $15 per player, paid on the first competition evening.

      On Thursday evenings, seven courts are reserved for league matches.

      Registration

      • Teams can register in the levels Open, Advanced, Intermediate and Fun.
      • Each poule consists of six teams. For some levels two poules may be formed.
      • Placement in poules is based on the combined individual ranking of both players.
      • If necessary, a seventh team may be added to a poule.

      Format

      • Each team plays one match against every other team in their poule.
      • Teams play one match per competition evening, occasionally two.
      • Matches are best of three sets. At 1–1, the third set is a super tiebreak.

      Competition evenings

      • Matches follow the “followed by” principle. The first matches start at 5:00 PM; the next match on a court starts as soon as the previous one ends.
      • All times are approximate. Teams must be ready to play to avoid delays.
      • Teams must be at the court at least 30 minutes before their scheduled start.
      • A team not present within 10 minutes after the court becomes available loses the first set 6–3.
      • A team not present within 15 minutes after the court becomes available loses the match 6–3, 6–3.

      Unable to play

      • Teams are expected on all competition evenings. At registration, each team may name one date on which they cannot play.
      • The final league day is excluded: attendance is required for semifinals and finals.
      • Each team may reschedule one match on its own initiative by finding an open slot and agreeing it with the opponents. Always tell the League Officials.
      • A rescheduling request must be made before 10:00 PM on the day before the match. After that, the result is recorded as 6–3, 6–3.
      • If no replacement date is found, the result is recorded as 6–3, 6–3.
      • Teams may use a substitute at most twice per season. The substitute must be of the same or a lower level and may not be registered in another team.
      • Opponents may refuse to play a substitute and ask to reschedule to a date when the original player is available.
      • With long-term injury or another external reason, a permanent substitute of the same or lower level may finish the season, approved by the League Officials and not registered in another team.

      Scoring

      League standings are determined by, in order:

      • Number of matches won
      • Sets won minus sets lost
      • Games won minus games lost
      • Head-to-head results

      Ranking points

      League results count towards the Bonaire Ranking System.

      Finals

      • For levels with two poules, semifinals and finals are played on the final league day between the #1 and #2 of each poule.
      • Quarterfinals are not played. The #3 and #4 of each poule receive quarterfinalist points.
      • For levels with one poule there are no finals. The final standings decide the points.

      Final note

      Beach Tennis Bonaire and the League Officials have the final say in any ruling.

      PanAm selection 2026

      How BTB selects the players who represent Bonaire at the Pan American Championships 2026.

      Standings: women (PDF)Standings: men (PDF)
      1. Objective

      1.1 This policy sets out the procedure for selecting and qualifying the players who will represent Bonaire at the Pan American Championships 2026 (PanAm). It aims for a fair, transparent and performance-based selection.

      1.2 This policy is final and binding and applies only to the selection for the Pan American Championships of 2026.

      1.3 This policy is carried out by the BTB board.

      2. Selection period

      2.1 The qualification period for all players runs from July 1, 2025 to July 1, 2026.

      2.2 At the end of this period, the board announces the selection.

      3. Selection criteria

      3.1 Selection is based only on the official national doubles ranking. The four highest-ranked female doubles players and the four highest-ranked male doubles players are selected. Only doubles results count; points from mixed doubles and singles do not.

      3.2 If two or more players have the same number of points at the end of the period, these tiebreaks apply in order:

      1. Best result in a recognised BTB tournament (highest round reached at the highest tournament level).
      2. Head-to-head results between the tied players during the qualification period, if any.
      3. Number of tournaments played during the qualification period.
      4. Decision by the BTB board, in consultation with the parties involved.
      4. Requirements for players

      4.1 Besides the performance criteria, players must meet these formal requirements:

      • Resided on Bonaire for at least one year at the time of selection.
      • A valid Sedula (ID) of Bonaire.
      • A valid passport.
      • Registered under the Bonairean flag with the ITF no later than August 1, 2026.
      • Comply with the vaccination requirements and recommendations for travel to the host country, as set out by the GGD. Show proof of the vaccinations or of an appointment before departure.
      • Accept to cover all costs of taking part, including travel and accommodation, unless BTB or another authorised body provides support.
      • Submit official documentation confirming availability within 15 days of selection.

      4.2 Selected players are expected to:

      • Represent the Beach Tennis Association in a sporting, professional and respectful way.
      • Take part in all official training sessions, team activities, meetings, media moments and other official events set by the board.
      • Comply with the rules of the association, the national federation and the championship organisers.
      • Have valid travel documents, visas and other required papers on time, unless the board says otherwise.
      5. Team composition

      5.1 At most two men's doubles teams, two women's doubles teams and two mixed doubles teams may represent Bonaire.

      5.2 The qualified players may decide together which two players form the first team and which the second, for both doubles and mixed doubles.

      5.3 If they cannot agree, the BTB board makes the final decision.

      5.4 Each National Association may enter at most 2 men's, 2 women's and 2 mixed doubles teams (the host nation may enter one extra team per event). A squad has up to 4 male and 4 female players. Only players in the men's or women's doubles may play mixed doubles. Players are nominated by their National Association and must be eligible to represent that nation. Each nation names a captain, playing or non-playing, who is responsible on site.

      6. Confirmation of participation

      6.1 Selected players confirm within 15 days of the announcement with the form "BTB Registration Form: Selected Player Pan American Championships 2026", meet all formal requirements and provide the documents.

      6.2 Without timely confirmation, or if a player declines or does not meet the requirements, the spot goes to the next eligible player on the ranking, down to the 10th ranked player.

      7. Exceptions

      7.1 In exceptional cases such as injury or force majeure, the BTB board may appoint a replacement, within or outside the ranking order, in consultation.

      7.2 In serious personal circumstances, such as the death of a close family member or serious illness, the board may deviate from the procedure in consultation with the player.

      7.3 If a highly ranked player is suspended, behaves inappropriately or breaks the BTB or ITF code of conduct, the board may exclude that player from selection, whatever their ranking.

      7.4 In all these cases the board makes a well-considered decision in consultation with the parties involved, documents it with reasons and communicates it in writing.

      About BTB

      Beach Tennis Bonaire is the driving force behind the island's liveliest sports community. Our mission: everyone who lives on or visits Bonaire can play this fast, spectacular sport every day, free of charge.

      Six courts, kept right

      BTB works to boost well-being and social connection across the island with a professional venue where people meet and stay active.

      Six permanent courts with high-quality sand and professional boundary lines, night lighting for evening matches, shade along the side of the courts, and secure court enclosures. We keep improving them.

      Events

      Monthly tournaments, competitions, trainings and other beach tennis activities. Players pay a small contribution to take part.

      Ranking

      An official ranking since 2017. Everyone who plays a ranking tournament is placed on the list. See the rankings

      The fastest growing sport on the island

      Want to jump on the wagon? Support BTB and grow your visibility at the same time.

      BTB runs under Stichting Sport Promotie Bonaire, the Foundation for Sport Promotion on Bonaire. Its aim: keep beach tennis accessible for every inhabitant and visitor of Bonaire, preferably free, gratis, por nada.

      To do that while the sport keeps growing, the foundation depends entirely on fundraising, sponsoring and small contributions from tournament players. Every dollar goes to that goal: loan rackets and balls, nets, lines, lights, fences, sun shades, a terrace and, not to forget, sand.
